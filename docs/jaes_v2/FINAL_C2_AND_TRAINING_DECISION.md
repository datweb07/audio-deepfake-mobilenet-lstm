## Mathematical equivalence and falsification

For class sign y and ordered logits z_ab and z_ba, let m = (z_ab + z_ba)/2 and d = (z_ab − z_ba)/2. Squared d is one quarter of ordinary squared logit consistency. For decreasing logistic loss ℓ, ℓ(ym − |d|) equals max(ℓ(yz_ab), ℓ(yz_ba)). The worse view receives the gradient away from ties. Equal-view BCE averages these losses. Class-dependent coefficients on the BCE or consistency terms produce weighted BCE or class-aware consistency. Group-DRO takes the maximum over group-average risk, while constrained risk uses separately defined acceptable risks. These distinctions in aggregation matter for a comparator, but the operations themselves are established.

A detached independent target d* would give opposite logit gradients proportional to d − d*. Neither the sealed scores nor the matched waveform components define d*. A target inferred from the same detector is circular. A severity target can be reproducible without being spoof-specific. A class-only target can encode the empirical score drift and dataset shortcuts. Any future evidence would need a directly observable cue, an intervention on that cue, matched nuisance changes and support in unseen train-derived speaker folds across architectures. This sprint found no candidate eligible for optimization.

A paired order-label swap preserves the magnitude of each contrast and destroys directional association. It is suitable for testing a signed association after blocking source and speaker, not for evaluating an unsigned worst-view loss. Matched SNR, gain, bandwidth, clipping and codec-delay controls test whether waveform nuisance can account for the score shift. Identity/lossless and matched-energy sham controls would test cue specificity once a cue exists. Any mechanism selection uses train-only speaker-held-out fixtures. The existing development nuisance screen is descriptive and cannot serve as confirmation.

The matched component run and development residualization are complete. Both classes shift in the same mean score direction under the order contrast. The raw FAKE-minus-REAL mean contrast changes sign between ERM and channel, and residualization yields AUC 0.538 and 0.432 in the declared direction. This does not identify a stable signed target. A descriptive order effect remains, including adverse A06 and clean B outcomes. [Executed analysis](FINAL_SCIENTIFIC_DIAGNOSTIC.md)
