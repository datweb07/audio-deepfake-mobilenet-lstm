## Precision and feasibility

Development size was chosen for complete cells and resource-bounded diagnostics: 24 FAKE per system, 96 REAL; four original-source speakers, not 96 independent synthetic voices. Under an ideal independent-binomial approximation, 24 observations have worst-case 95% half-width about .20; 96 about .10. These are illustrative planning values, not achieved cluster-bootstrap precision or a power calculation.

For a paired order difference D in [-1,1], a conservative independent-unit planning bound is half-width <=1.96/sqrt(n). A target .05 would require approximately 1,537 genuinely independent units under that bound. A two-group interaction needs additional variance/group allocation and source-cluster estimates, not four speakers replicated many times. Future sample size must use realistic pilot variance, source-level design effects and a declared practical effect; the present cohort cannot justify a powered confirmatory claim.

Before a new study: secure source/license and release, seal exact IDs with source/speaker grouping, audit clean pairing and transformation metadata, lock factors/estimand/multiplicity/thresholds, calculate sample size and preserve an untouched corpus. Do not treat unused MLAAD-tiny utterances sharing these same source speakers as independent-corpus confirmation.
