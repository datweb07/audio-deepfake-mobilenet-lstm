# Phase 9 — Nearest prior art and research decision

**Branch B · DATA_BLOCKER · NOVELTY_NOT_ESTABLISHED.** The Phase 8 stress ranking change motivates a search for mechanisms. The B clean and A05/A06 regressions invalidate a blanket robustness claim. A targeted checkpoint guard is useful engineering and overlaps established constrained optimization.

## Single research redirection

Continue with frozen-protocol ERM, matched channel augmentation, standard Group-DRO where needed and guarded selection as controls. Before any C2 loss, test whether an independently defined generator cue survives ordered channels after controlling for source identity and nuisance. For source u, generator g and aligned descriptor h, define d_g(u)=h(G_g(u))−h(u) and d_g^T(u)=h(T(G_g(u)))−h(T(u)). Test projected survival S_g(u,T)=〈d_g^T,d_g〉/||d_g||² only with a preregistered non-negligible denominator and alignment tolerance. Subtract survival under an independently matched-energy, matched-band sham perturbation and report uncertainty grouped by original source and generator. The candidate measurement is conditional on verified parallel lineage, channel determinism and a descriptor defined independently of detector error. Generic SNR/energy, gain, frequency masking, shuffled lineage and same-condition source controls can falsify specificity. A method claim would additionally require improvement over ERM, equal-view augmentation, ordinary consistency, severity weighting, and Group-DRO with equal update and view budgets, including ablations of cue, matching, gating and constraints. A low-cost MobileNet training-only cue is feasible in principle once measurements exist. Costs are unmeasured, aligned audio and extra views increase training cost, and no inference savings have been demonstrated. No loss or candidate training launched.

C1 is supporting evidence for order-sensitive class/attack error, with confirmatory source lineage still needed. C2 is unvalidated. C3 selective inference is deferred until calibration rights, untouched validation and explicit risk–coverage with cost.
