# External evaluation and journal indexing

## ASVspoof 2021 DF

The official Zenodo record has four compressed parts of 8.6–8.7 GB, totaling 34.5 GB. No DF archive was found in the repository data directory. The current resource snapshot gives 20.9 GB free on D and 9.6 GB on C. Full staging exceeds either drive before extraction. Extracted footprint and per-part membership remain to be verified. The published DF metadata describe source corpus, speaker, trial ID, codec, attack, class, subset and vocoder. DF includes ASVspoof 2019 LA evaluation material alongside other source pools. Different release years therefore do not guarantee independent utterances. Official audio is FLAC packaged in the tarballs. Local decoder behavior, sample rates for the selected items and exact audio lineage are unverified. The official record exposes no named license in the retrieved Rights section. Confirm the distribution terms before any redistribution or use outside the verified scope. [Zenodo release](https://zenodo.org/records/4835108) · [Official keys and maps](https://www.asvspoof.org/index2021.html) · [Official key schema](https://github.com/asvspoof-challenge/2021/blob/main/eval-package/README.md) · [Challenge proceedings](https://www.isca-archive.org/asvspoof_2021/yamagishi21_asvspoof.pdf)

The two official metadata packages were retrieved without audio. The key has 611,829 unique trial IDs. All 159,696 VCTK-map rows and 452,133 VCC-map rows join to the key. The 2019 map covers the 640 train, 368 development and 1,088 locked held-out IDs. The VCTK map identifies 4,455 DF rows sharing a named parent utterance with the locked held-out cohort and 159,696 sharing a speaker identity from that cohort. It identifies no parent or speaker intersections against the 640 train or 368 development records within that comparable namespace. TTS rows lack parent audio IDs and cannot be cleared by the same test. The VCC and VCTK string namespaces are distinct and lack a verified crosswalk in this audit. A VCC-only candidate has nominal support, including 770 bona fide and 49,467 spoof official eval rows in the nocodec stratum before lineage, duplication and archive-member checks. The mapping database ships ODC-By 1.0. This metadata license does not establish the terms governing redistribution of the audio contents. [Machine-readable audit](../../outputs/jaes_v2/supervisor_major_revision/df_metadata_audit.json)
**Decision.** Full DF is blocked by storage. A bounded subset remains blocked by source lineage, license verification and packaging feasibility. A possible design is up to 256 bona fide plus 256 spoofed official eval sources, prespecified before inference, with corpus and native codec strata, fixed threshold 0.5, four sealed models, and one native view per source. This is 2,048 forwards, approximately 3.5 minutes at the measured clean development per-item median as a compute-only scale marker. Archive access, actual durations, decoding, integrity checking and variant support are additional. Select only after joining official 2019/2021 mapping metadata and excluding shared parent utterances, normalized IDs, shared speakers where identifiable and identical audio. If the joined clean candidate lacks both classes or verified lineage, report a shift diagnostic only, or defer. No audio was downloaded or scored. MLAAD-tiny already informed development.

## JAES quartile requirement

JAES is the Audio Engineering Society peer-reviewed journal, eISSN 1549-4950. A public institutional JCR June 2024 export lists Acoustics under SCIE with JIF quartile Q3 and JCI quartile Q4. The Wageningen library display reports a 2024 Scopus Q1 listing. These are dated and use different metrics. Current category-specific quartiles were not independently confirmed directly in the Clarivate JCR and Scopus source dashboards. Ask the supervisor whether the requirement means exact Q3 or Q3-or-better and which database, category and year count. Obtain the institutional JCR/Scopus source export for the accepted criterion before finalizing venue eligibility.
