# Phase 5 C2 novelty gate — 28 October 2026

**No international methodological novelty established.** Implemented baselines are controls, not three relabeled contributions. A passing software test never upgrades novelty status.

[RawBoost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.04433) models waveform nuisance through several noise/distortion processes. A small AWGN/Opus bank is not its reproduction or a new alternative proven superior to it. A proper future comparison must use its released recipe and comparable compute.

[PCL-NET preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07216), submitted 5 October 2026, explicitly uses paired original/neural-codec representation consistency with a large pretrained encoder. Ordinary clean/distorted consistency is therefore direct overlap. Our conventional Opus perturbations also do not represent its neural-codec resynthesis setting.

[DK-CAST](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10791-025-09746-4) describes multi-level teacher supervision, codec-aware weighting and compression-agnostic embedding alignment. A weighted combination of BCE/KD/consistency cannot be presented as distinct merely because the student backbone is MobileNet. Its reported results are author claims, not independently reproduced baselines here; no cross-paper numeric superiority claim is made.

RADAR is a transfer/evaluation precedent, not a new paired-generator-order dataset. The Phase 3 metadata audit does not constitute executing its baselines. The prior-art review remains targeted rather than exhaustive: a full recent ICASSP/Interspeech/JAES method search and reproducible strong-baseline implementation are formal Phase 6 gates. Missing comparison to these bodies of work prevents any exhaustive novelty claim.

Scientific upgrade requires a specified technical difference, controlled benefit over ERM + gain augmentation + strong channel augmentation + equal-view consistency, a bounded clean-risk trade-off, ablations, at least three training seeds and genuinely unused external evaluation. If simple established methods solve the diagnosed problem, retain it as engineering/validation and reject a fabricated methodological contribution. C1 supporting, C2 under investigation, C3 deferred remain the correct claim map.

Additional targeted primary-source checks: [Generalizable Detection of Audio Deepfakes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01750) studies pretrained backbones, augmentation and losses, so comparing such ingredients is established research. [ADD-C communication benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12423), accepted EUSIPCO 2025 and revised June 2026, already combines codec/transmission robustness evaluation with augmentation. These are further reasons not to claim a new robustness benchmark merely from our perturbation grid. A search also located *I Can Hear You: Selective Robust Training for Deepfake Audio Detection* (ICLR 2025), but [its primary OpenReview page](https://openreview.net/forum?id=2GcR9bO620) and PDF returned a browser-verification challenge. Its selective mechanism remains an explicit unresolved prior-art gate for the proposed retention/reliability direction; title-level discovery is not a completed technical comparison. No new selective objective is authorized on that basis.
