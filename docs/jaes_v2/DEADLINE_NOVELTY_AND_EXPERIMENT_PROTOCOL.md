## Primary method comparison

## Candidate A · Worth testing as an empirical mechanism, training NO-GO

Let (T_{ab}) denote noise then codec, (T_{ba}) the reverse, (s) a FAKE logit, (y\in\{-1,+1\}), (m=(s_{ab}+s_{ba})/2) and (d=(s_{ab}-s_{ba})/2). A proposed signed supervision term would need a target (d^*(x,y)) measured independently of the current detector. With a detached target, (L_o=(d-d^*)^2) yields logit derivatives (d-d^*) for (ab) and (-(d-d^*)) for (ba), moving the views in opposite directions. (d^*=0) gives ordinary logit consistency. A trainable score-derived target without independent restrictions admits trivial self-agreement. A class-only target can encode order-associated corpus score bias rather than forensic evidence. A robust margin surrogate (ℓ(ym-|d|)), for decreasing (ℓ), equals (max{ℓ(ys_{ab}),ℓ(ys_{ba})}). Its gradient selects the currently worse view, with a subgradient at ties. It is within-record worst-view optimization, distinct in granularity from max over population group means and still a standard robust objective. A contrastive loss between view embeddings similarly requires a new reason for its positive and negative assignments.

Executed descriptive screen. Among 112 A REAL sources, only noise→codec produced a false alarm on 43 ERM sources versus 19 channel sources. The reverse-only count was zero for both. Among 112 A FAKE sources, only codec→noise missed on 48 ERM versus 17 channel sources. Noise→codec-only missed on one ERM source and zero channel sources. This class-dependent consequence of score shifts motivates the falsification above. It cannot separate cue degradation from preprocessing, threshold effects or corpus shortcuts by itself. ## Candidate B · REJECT_CANDIDATE_B

A cue-retention target would require detector-independent measurement, verified alignment, measurable positive controls, cue-knockout negatives and matched-energy/bandwidth sham changes. Test per attack against SNR, RMS, generic spectral distance and shuffle controls. Phase 9 proposed projected residual survival on known parallel source and generated counterparts. The current data do not identify that residual, and score gap or embedding distance cannot substitute for it. DK-CAST and PCL-NET already cover close proxy supervision. No loss, estimate of model overhead or training pilot is justified for B. Revisit only if the measurement and provenance prerequisites are satisfied.

## Statistical and held-out protocol

1. Development comparisons use equal-weight macro ROC-AUC over noise5, noise→codec and codec→noise for stratum A. Guard at fixed score 0.5. Use A clean FA and miss, B clean FA, and A05/A06 stress misses. Retain denominators and exact error counts. Three views of one source are repeated observations. No two-class metrics for B or an attack-only subgroup.
2. For prospective replication, report the three matched seed deltas, their range and exact per-seed guard decisions. Use paired speaker-cluster bootstrap with fixed seed 91109 and 10,000 draws as descriptive uncertainty for the fixed-cohort model contrast. Sample within stratum and retain all source/view scores for a sampled speaker. For sparse attack coverage report support and resampling instability. Show all endpoints and label subgroup and order diagnostics secondary. Do not infer seed-level statistical reliability from three pairs or use row-level resampling.
