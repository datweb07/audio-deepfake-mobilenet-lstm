# Author checks before JAES submission

- Confirm the conference's official title, venue, dates, paper ID, acceptance notice and presentation status. Check the proceedings entry and publication date, and supply the DOI and citation if assigned. The ICAIN-804 match in the repository is provisional.
- Retrieve the executed copyright or licence agreement. Check which text, figures, tables and other material may be reused, record any permissions and disclose the earlier manuscript and its relationship to the journal submission.
- Confirm the journal author list, order, affiliations, corresponding author and email, ORCIDs, author consent and biographies of up to 150 words. Complete the official entry point before submission. [AES author guidelines](https://aes.org/publications/journal-author-guidelines/)
- Confirm the scope and nature of UEH support for the journal work and supply any grant identifier. V7 restores the wording used in the conference acknowledgement at the author's request.
- Review the substantive AI disclosure retained by the official entry point. Confirm tool name, provider, model, version, dates and scope of drafting and figure or code assistance. The authors should verify the manuscript and its evidence. [AES AI disclosure requirements](https://aes.org/publications/journal-author-guidelines/)
- Verify the institution's required database, category and year, then record the corresponding JCR or Scopus evidence. Keep database-specific rankings with their category and year.
- Confirm with the supervisor whether R08 can remain an explicit limitation. A future external evaluation needs a qualified corpus and an approved evaluation lock before scoring.
