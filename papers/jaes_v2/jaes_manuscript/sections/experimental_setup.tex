\section{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:setup}
\subsection{Cohorts and conditions}
The resource-bounded medium cohort includes 640 ASVspoof 2019 LA training recordings, balanced between REAL and FAKE, and attack IDs A01--A06. Development A consists of 112 REAL and 112 FAKE recordings from seven speakers. Development B adds 144 REAL-only recordings from nine speakers. The registered audit checks disjoint training and development speaker IDs. The same development sources are used for the two independently initialized blocks, so the blocks measure sensitivity to training, rather than to a new test population.

The seven scored views per model are clean, two gain controls, Noise5, Opus64, and the two reversed chains. The stress endpoint uses Noise5 and the chains. In the chains, a fixed-absolute 10~dB noise plan and the 24~kb/s Opus encoder are shared across order. Noise5 and Opus64 are separate conditions. The matched standalone Noise10 and Opus24 diagnostic was performed for seed 9107 only. Table~\ref{tab:design} summarizes the fixed design.

The medium cohort was chosen to permit matched CPU runs with balanced class support. It has 320 REAL and 320 FAKE sources. The full training partition contains 2,580 REAL and 22,800 FAKE recordings, so a comparison with that partition would change both training size and class mixture.

\subsection{Paired uncertainty and efficiency}
For each seed, differences are channel minus ERM. Percentile intervals use 10,000 paired speaker-cluster bootstrap draws with seed 91109. A and B are sampled separately. Within a sampled speaker, all sources and their views retain their paired model scores. This preserves source and speaker dependence and avoids treating transformed views as independent trials. Mean and range across the two blocks are descriptive. They do not estimate a population confidence interval over random initializations.

The four runs used a Windows CPU environment. Optimizer and validation durations come from run logs and may include repeated work after interruption. The registered evaluation policy preserves the 0.5 threshold and excludes calibration from model selection.

\subsection{Design controls and support}
Development A and B play different roles. A supplies both classes for ROC-AUC, EER and fixed-threshold risks. B adds a second, REAL-only speaker stratum for false-alarm sensitivity. Pooling B into A would change the implicit weighting of speakers and the set of REAL--FAKE pairs. The two strata are retained separately. Counts in the mixed-class stratum are supported by 112 original recordings per class. The three stress views contribute 336 outcomes per class but still derive from those same recordings.

Within each seed block, both fitted models see the same registered development sources and transformed waveforms. Between seeds, the independent initializations vary the fitted functions, but the evaluation cohort stays fixed. Thus there are two kinds of pairing. Source pairing controls the input to a between-method comparison, and seed blocking controls initialization and source order during training. Neither kind of pairing gives two independent samples of the speaker population. All attacks A01--A06 are represented in training. The attack rows therefore show differences across represented attack IDs under stress.

The order experiment inserts the same fixed-absolute noise realization before or after the codec, with the same nominal clean-referenced signal-to-noise ratio. The achieved characteristics after compression need not be equal. Treating achieved SNR or bandwidth as equal would erase part of the intervention. The controls support comparison of those specific pipelines and do not simulate packet loss, device reverberation, proprietary enhancement or a complete communications stack.

\subsection{Interpretation of the resampling intervals}
Cluster resampling keeps all observations from each sampled speaker together \cite{field}. In each replicate the same speaker weights apply to ERM and channel within a stratum. A speaker drawn more than once contributes the corresponding repeated cluster weight. The analysis recalculates the contrast under those weights and takes the percentile limits over valid draws. Repeated views of a source and paired arm predictions therefore stay linked. For rank metrics, the class support requirement is evaluated within the resampling design. The evidence records the valid draw count so that interval interpretation can account for any undefined draws.

Seven and nine speakers provide limited empirical support for percentile intervals. Large changes in one speaker can influence the interval and can also affect which attack cases are represented in a resample. The intervals condition on the fitted models and do not incorporate a hypothetical new training seed. The differences across seeds are summarized descriptively, and the secondary attack and speaker comparisons are used to localize effects rather than to claim a family of discoveries.

\subsection{Evidence boundaries}
The prospective comparison includes the registered score orientation, 0.5 operating rule, fixed budget, clean-loss checkpoint selection and stress conditions. The paired records define the unit for contrasting methods. The separate Noise10 and Opus24 runs for 9107 were added as diagnostics on the same fitted models and development sources. They help characterize departures from additive output behavior, with no independent new-cohort replication. The interpretation of component nonadditivity and nuisance adjustment therefore stays distinct from that of the prospective augmentation comparison.

\subsection{Locked Held-Out Evaluation}\label{sec:heldout-design}
The primary held-out subset comprises 1,088 recordings from 67 speakers in the official ASVspoof 2019 LA countermeasure evaluation partition \cite{asv19}. A deterministic hash ranking and speaker round-robin selection with seed 91109 yields 256 REAL recordings and 64 FAKE recordings for each attack A07 through A19. The audit reconciled all 71,237 official trials with the archive and found no shared source IDs, speaker IDs, or audio SHA-256 digests between the selected subset and the registered training, development, and calibration manifests. Complete generator and original-utterance lineage remains unverified.

The lock specifies four development-selected checkpoints and four conditions per source: clean, Noise5, Noise10 followed by Opus24, and Opus24 followed by Noise10. All 17,408 predictions were completed. The sigmoid score is oriented toward FAKE and scores of at least 0.5 are assigned to FAKE. Macro stressed ROC-AUC averages the three stress-condition AUCs. Clean and stressed error rates use class-conditional denominators, and the stressed denominator counts three correlated views per source. Held-out contrasts retain within-seed source pairing and use the same speaker-bootstrap settings on the held-out speakers. Development A and B refer only to the earlier development strata.

All source--condition--model cells were checked against the locked identities. Per-source score shards retain complete model and condition sets, and their hashes were verified before analysis. The registered metrics and uncertainty outputs were recomputed from the committed predictions. The selected sources, models, transformations, and threshold were fixed before scoring. The attack-balanced subset has different support from development A and B, so differences in effect magnitude across partitions are descriptive.
