\section{Conclusion}

In RQ1, channel augmentation boosted the stressed ranking in all the three 640-development blocks and the original locked within-release comparisons. The relatively smaller ranking gain at 1,280 in seed 9207 accompanied fewer stressed FAKE misses and more stressed REAL false alarms at threshold 0.5. The clean-BCE-selected Channel checkpoint violated both clean-risk guards.

For RQ2, the A05/A06 miss comparisons inverted between the two size protocols in seed 9207 while the initializations varied in 640. The original held out pairs still suffered from clean FAKE misses and stressed misses on A17-A19. For RQ3, inversion of noise and codec order affected the decision for the same recordings and the REAL order contrast depended on the training protocol used. Reporting ranking along with classwise risks, attack counts, and source-specific transitions reveals such differences. In the size comparison, one seed is used together with more optimizer steps and shared development sources. On In-the-Wild, Channel improved paired ranking in all three fitted seed blocks, while all six AUCs remained below 0.5 and REAL false-alarm rates stayed high. FAKE misses increased slightly in one seed. The external diagnostic used native audio and leaves the within-release order interpretation and its unresolved forensic mechanism unchanged. The results motivate reporting ranking alongside classwise risks, attack effects and variability across fitted protocols and datasets.
