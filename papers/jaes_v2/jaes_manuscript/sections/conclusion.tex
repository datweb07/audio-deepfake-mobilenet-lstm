\section{Conclusion}
Matched channel augmentation improved stressed ranking in two development blocks for a lightweight speech deepfake detector. Aggregate improvements coexisted with more A05 and A06 misses at a fixed threshold, and REAL-only false alarms changed in different directions. Source-linked processing-order comparisons exposed error asymmetries that pooled ranking summaries conceal. These findings support explicit class, attack and condition reporting when assessing lightweight countermeasures. A locked within-release evaluation is the next test of the observed trade-offs.
