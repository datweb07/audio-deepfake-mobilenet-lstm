\section{Conclusion}
Channel augmentation improved stressed ranking and reduced pooled stressed FAKE misses in both matched initialization blocks on development and on the locked within-release evaluation. The held-out results also exposed a cost in clean FAKE sensitivity and additional stressed errors on A17--A19. The classwise error pattern depended on seed, condition, and partition.

The matched training comparison, source-linked predictions, and paired speaker-cluster analysis make those differences inspectable. Reversing noise and codec order continued to produce different REAL and FAKE error transitions on the held-out sources. These findings support evaluation of lightweight countermeasures with ranking, classwise risk, attack support, and same-source channel diagnostics reported together. Broader generalization across corpora and model families, and the forensic mechanism underlying the response, remain unresolved.
