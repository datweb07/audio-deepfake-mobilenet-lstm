\section{Conclusion}
Channel augmentation improved stressed ranking and reduced pooled stressed FAKE misses in both matched initialization blocks on development and on the locked within-release evaluation. Clean FAKE sensitivity declined on held-out, and the descriptive attack analysis found additional stressed misses on A17--A19. Under noise followed by Opus in 9207, channel reduced REAL false alarms while increasing FAKE misses at the same operating point.

Reversing noise and codec order produced different REAL and FAKE error transitions on the same held-out recordings. The measured behavior supports reporting rank metrics alongside classwise risks and testing the two channel orders when they are relevant to the application. The evidence concerns one architecture, two seed blocks and one corpus release. Transfer beyond that scope and the forensic mechanism underlying the response remain unresolved.
