\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:limits}

Nested training sets are balanced and compute-constrained. Training on full-foundation data is out of scope for the current experiment design and would alter the class composition to be 89.83\% FAKE rather than 50\% FAKE. The comparison of sizes makes use of only one initialization and one nested expansion that doubles updates from 840 to 1,680. Questions about size effects at fixed compute and robustness under different draws of the cohort remain open. Both A and B have limited speaker resources; development attack sets contain 18 or 19 sources.

The three development initialization blocks use the identical development data and checkpointing rule. The identical partition is used for checkpointing selection and development analysis reporting. The results are dependent on the predetermined decision threshold and checkpoint used. Specifically, the guard failure refers to the selected checkpoint comparison. It does not mean that all checkpoints or epochs would fail.

The matched architecture design involved retraining just a single architecture. Preprocessing, noise rescaling, and codec reaction can independently modify score difference without affecting forensic cue preservation. At present, no independently validated forensic target exists for use in our experiments. The locked within-release evaluation used four fits of one architecture trained on 640 recordings each. Some attacks share algorithms with earlier partitions. An external corpus with known audio labels, source lineage, and distinct speakers/utterances must be available to test cross-corpus generalization.

\subsection{Internal and external validity}

The signal intervention manages the designed processing and the matched inputs, yet the clean population decides which speakers and synthesis instances are permitted to participate. In the case of release, reuse of attack families makes it impossible for there to be any simple unseen-generator interpretation. Additionally, the detector picks checkpoints based on the clean development BCE, which may combine the clean performance with the selection data. The selection registration precludes any retrospective modification of the stress effects, without altering the dependence. Other operating points require an independent locked design.

The source is the unit used to link ordered views, while the speaker is the resampling unit. The model receives correlated transformations of the same speech signal at evaluation time. More views do not substitute for more speakers. Specifically, attack-level confidence intervals depend on a small number of clusters of sources and speakers. Another specification of the bootstrap, another sample, or another leave-one-speaker-out approach could affect the size of the interval.

\subsection{Engineering scope}

Reproducibility is guaranteed by execution integrity, deterministic replay verification, and artifact sealing in the case of the fitted comparisons. They do not guarantee that there is an equivalent number or latency behavior in the production version. These results have been measured using a CPU running in the registered environment and including training and validation operations. These results do not prove anything about mobile latency, energy consumption, streaming, or adversarial adaptation. The complete evaluation is based on a bounded and balanced set of attacks for each release.

\subsection{Construct validity and paired uncertainty}

A FAKE score is an output of the classifier. It will be affected by transformation if the latter changes the spectral structure, the temporal texture, the signal scale or the range of the preprocessing input. In the absence of an independent definition of the cue and intervention, the difference in scores cannot differentiate between the transformation of evidence and of sensitivity to that evidence.

The estimates from within-seed pairing correspond to a test population that is shared and rely on the registered clusters of speakers. The chosen clean checkpoint is an additional element of variation since different realizations of the training set could lead to choosing a different epoch. Differences between three blocks serve as robustness checks but cannot provide any reliable estimation of distribution of the results with respect to seed, architecture, and/or corpus. Thus, for each development contrast separately, mean and sample standard deviation are reported.

The held-out test sample consists of 64 sources for each FAKE attack and 256 REAL sources from 67 speakers. The choice of quota sampling differs from the official partition in terms of the weight of attacks. The attack intervals are pointwise and do not address multiplicity correction. Selection of A17-A19 and breakdown of particular mistakes was an afterthought and therefore any analysis of subgroups is exploratory, even when particular intervals exclude zero. These sources were initially rated with the fixed design. The choice of a model on these scores will erase the distinction.
